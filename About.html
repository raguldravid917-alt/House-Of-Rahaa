About House Of Rahaa

Rahaa House (Rahaa Associates) is a Chennai-based, ISO 9001:2015 certified real estate developer, specializing in land development, layout promotion, and property consulting.

With over 14 years of dedicated service in the industry, we pride ourselves on high standards of transparency, integrity, and professionalism.

Operating from our spacious office in Maduravoyal, Chennai, Rahaa House has successfully completed more than 110 residential townships and delivered over 25,000 plots to satisfied customers.

Our clientele includes government employees, business professionals, and NRIs across India. Our commitment to delivering plots with clear titles, legal transparency, and top-quality service has made us a trusted name in real estate.

At Rahaa House, we are dedicated to helping you find your perfect property with confidence and ease.
