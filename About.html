About us

Built on trust, clarity & consistency

Rahaa House (Rahaa Associates) is a Chennai-based ISO 9001:2015 certified real estate developer specialising in layout development and property consulting.

Operating from our spacious office in Maduravoyal, House Of Rahaa has successfully completed more than 10 residential townships and over 100 layouts. Across these projects we have delivered more than 25,000 plots to satisfied customers.

We serve a diverse client base including government employees, business professionals, NRIs and first-time home buyers. Our core strength is transparent documentation, clear communication and a no-shortcut approach to approvals and legal checks.

Every project is carefully picked for its connectivity, social infrastructure and long-term appreciation potential so that your investment grows with the city.

What defines House Of Rahaa

  • ISO 9001:2015 certified processes and documentation.
  • End-to-end support from site visit to registration.
  • Only CMDA / DTCP approved layouts with clear title.
  • Friendly team that explains every document in simple Tamil/English.
  • Long-term relationship – many clients buy second and third plots with us.

Our vision is to make land ownership simple, safe and joyful for every middle-class family dreaming of their own home.
