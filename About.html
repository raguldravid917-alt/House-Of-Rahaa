About us

Built on trust, clarity & consistency

Rahaa House (Rahaa Associates) is a Chennai-based ISO 9001:2015 certified real estate developer specialising in land development, layout promotion and property consulting.

Rahaa House (Rahaa Associates) is a Chennai-based, ISO 9001:2015 certified real estate developer specializing in land development, layout promotion, and property consulting. With over 14 years of dedicated service, we pride ourselves on high standards of transparency and professionalism.

Operating from our spacious office in Maduravoyal, Chennai, Rahaa House has successfully completed more than 10 residential townships and over 100 layouts, delivering more than 25,000 plots to satisfied customers.

We serve a diverse client base, including government employees, business professionals, and international clients from various states across India. Our commitment to providing clear titles, legal transparency, and timely service has made us a trusted choice with a strong record of repeat customers.

At Rahaa House, we are dedicated to helping you find your perfect property with confidence and ease.

What defines House Of Rahaa

  • ISO 9001:2015 certified processes and documentation.
  • Only CMDA / DTCP approved layouts with clear title.
  • Friendly team explaining documents in simple Tamil / English.
  • End-to-end support from site visit to registration.
  • Strong base of repeat and referral customers.

Our vision is to make land ownership simple, safe and joyful for every middle-class family dreaming of their own home in and around Chennai.
